# Blog style guide
- Structure: one H1 title containing the focus keyword; a short intro (focus keyword within the first 100 words); at least 4 H2 sections (focus keyword in at least one H2); a conclusion with a call to action.
- Sentences average under 20 words; paragraphs of at most 4 sentences. Use bullet lists where they help scanning.
- Back every statistic or claim with a Markdown link to one of the provided sources. Never invent numbers, quotes or URLs.
- Keyword density 0.5–2.5%; use natural variations, never stuff.
- Write for the stated audience in the stated tone. No filler openers.
